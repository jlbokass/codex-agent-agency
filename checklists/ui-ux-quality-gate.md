# UI/UX Quality Gate Checklist

Use this checklist before validating any page, component, landing page, dashboard, form, or interface redesign.

## Goal

Ensure the interface is clear, responsive, accessible, visually distinctive, and not generic.

---

## UX Clarity

- The main user goal is immediately understandable.
- The primary CTA is visually clear.
- Secondary actions are not competing with the main CTA.
- The page answers the user's main questions in the right order.
- The user journey is logical and friction is reduced.
- Important information is not hidden too low on the page.

---

## Visual Hierarchy

- Titles, subtitles, body text, and CTAs have clear hierarchy.
- Important content visually stands out.
- Sections are not all visually equal.
- The eye naturally moves through the page.
- There is enough contrast between key elements.

---

## Layout Quality

Avoid:
- repetitive card grids
- generic Bootstrap layouts
- predictable AI-generated sections
- excessive symmetry
- identical spacing everywhere
- boring vertical stacking

Prefer:
- editorial layouts
- controlled asymmetry
- immersive sections
- strong opening section
- varied section rhythm
- premium spacing
- clear visual storytelling

---

## Typography

- Font sizes are intentional.
- Line heights are readable.
- Text blocks are not too wide.
- Headings are visually strong.
- Typography supports the brand mood.
- Body text remains comfortable on mobile.

---

## Spacing & Rhythm

- Spacing is consistent but not mechanical.
- Sections have breathing room.
- Related elements are visually grouped.
- Unrelated elements are clearly separated.
- Mobile spacing is not just a compressed desktop layout.

---

## Color & Atmosphere

- Colors support the brand mood.
- Contrast is sufficient.
- Accent colors are used intentionally.
- The palette does not feel random.
- Backgrounds add depth without hurting readability.

---

## Components

- Buttons are visually consistent.
- Cards, forms, badges, and navigation feel coherent.
- Interactive states are defined.
- Hover/focus states are polished.
- Components do not look copied from a default template.

---

## Responsive Design

- Mobile layout is intentionally designed.
- CTAs remain easy to reach on mobile.
- Text remains readable on small screens.
- Images and sections scale cleanly.
- No layout breaks at common breakpoints.
- Touch targets are large enough.

---

## Accessibility Basics

- Semantic HTML is used where possible.
- Images have appropriate alt text.
- Contrast is acceptable.
- Focus states are visible.
- Forms have labels and helpful errors.
- The page remains understandable without animation.

---

## Motion & Interactions

- Animations support hierarchy or comprehension.
- Motion is subtle and purposeful.
- Hover effects are not excessive.
- Reduced-motion preferences are respected when relevant.
- No animation hides essential content.

---

## Anti-Generic Review

Before approving, ask:

- Does this look like a default AI-generated layout?
- Could this be confused with a basic Bootstrap template?
- Is there a clear design point of view?
- Is there a memorable visual moment?
- Does the design support the client’s positioning?

If the answer is weak, propose a stronger visual direction before finalizing.

---

## Final UI/UX Validation Output

Return:

- UX clarity assessment
- visual hierarchy assessment
- responsive assessment
- accessibility assessment
- generic/template risk
- recommended improvements
- final approval status
